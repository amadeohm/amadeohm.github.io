I am an AI/ML engineer and currently I work as research assistant in the [Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning group](https://www.upf.edu/web/ai-ml/home) at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, working with [Pablo Moreno-Muñoz](https://pmorenoz.github.io/). I completed the Master in Intelligent Interactive Systems at UPF and will pursue a PhD under his supervision.

I am interested in the foundations of machine learning, particularly **Bayesian methods and their connections to information theory**. I want to understand how models learn and use that understanding to develop efficient, practically useful methods.

My interests also include self-supervised learning, continual learning, reinforcement learning, computer vision, and multi-agent systems.
