### MSc in Intelligent Interactive Systems · UPF

I pursued the Master in Intelligent Interactive Systems at Universitat Pompeu Fabra to deepen my understanding of intelligent systems and develop a foundation for research. Its [curriculum](https://www.upf.edu/web/iis/academic-information) brings together machine learning, autonomous systems, natural language interaction and web intelligence, with research methodology as a core component and opportunities to specialise in areas such as probabilistic modelling, computer vision and interactive technologies.

I particularly valued studying and collaborating with people whose backgrounds spanned physics, industrial engineering and audiovisual technologies. Working together meant encountering different ways of framing a problem and reasoning about possible solutions. That exchange of perspectives was an important part of my education.

The research component culminated in my [master's thesis on probabilistic prediction and information-theoretic operators](/en/research/predig/), supervised by Pablo Moreno-Muñoz. I investigated how information-theoretic quantities can help identify observations that reduce predictive uncertainty, connecting Bayesian modelling with questions in self-supervised learning.
